Farm Active · Poultry & Farm ERP

A 4-minute tour of a real ERP, in daily use by a poultry company in Indonesia. Built by Awad Luckhoo.

Multi-tenant Integrated accounting Spring Boot · Java Angular · Ionic · Capacitor MySQL JWT · 12 roles English / Bahasa Indonesia

Born on the farm. Built for any business.

Integrated accounting

Every sale, purchase, return, transfer and payment posts its own double-entry journal, with no bookkeeping by hand. Chart of accounts, monthly balances, a trial balance with period lock, profit and loss, a balance sheet that always balances, cash flow, receivables and payables ageing, and bank reconciliation with auto-match.

Multi-tenant, multi-site

Every company and every site keeps its own data on one platform. Staff sign in once, choose their site, and 12 roles each see only what they need.

Sell anything, on credit too

A point of sale for eggs, chickens, feed and every material, with PDF invoices. Returns against the original invoice, customer deposits, buy now and pay later, and account settlements for customers and suppliers.

Flocks & production

Register flocks, drag them into houses and move them as birds mature. Daily house surveys feed the daily report and the egg report.

Product assembly

The feed mill turns raw materials into finished feed, with the cost of every bag worked out from its recipe.

One codebase, every screen

Web, tablet and phone from a single Angular, Ionic and Capacitor app, in English or Bahasa Indonesia at one click.

AI-assisted review

A recent AI-assisted code review added deeper quality checks, fixed hidden issues and brought new features.
